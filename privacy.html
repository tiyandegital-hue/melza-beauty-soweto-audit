Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026. This page explains, in plain language, what data this audit dossier website collects and why.

What this site is

This is a static audit report prepared by Tiyan Digital for Melza Beauty Spa. It is a read-only document: there are no accounts, no forms that collect your details, and no checkout.

What we collect

  • Nothing you type. This site has no forms, so it collects no names, email addresses, or messages from you directly.
  • Basic server logs. Like almost every website, the hosting provider may record standard technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you open — for security and reliability. We do not use this to identify you.
  • No analytics, no advertising trackers. This site loads no third-party analytics, advertising scripts, or tracking pixels.

What loads from elsewhere

All fonts and images on this site are hosted with the site itself. The only links that leave this site are the ones you choose to click: links to melzabeautyspa.co.za, Fresha, and your email app (via the “Reply” button). Those services have their own privacy policies.

Images on this site

The spa photographs on this site are AI-generated illustrative samples, clearly captioned as such. They are not photographs of Melza Beauty Spa's premises, staff, or clients, and they depict no real person.

Contacting us

If you reply to the email this dossier arrived with, your reply goes to Tiyan Digital at contact@tiyandegital.com and is used only to discuss the audit and the fix plan. We do not sell or share contact details.

Your rights

If you would like a copy of anything we hold about you in connection with this dossier, or would like it deleted, email contact@tiyandegital.com and we will action it.
