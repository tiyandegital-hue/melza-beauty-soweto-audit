8 Discrepancies Costing You Bookings Every Single Week.

World-class botanical spa treatments let down by website fracture points.

Clients experience peaceful Meadowlands earth tones, warm herbal treatments, and specialised care in the treatment room. The website should carry that same care — right now it carries contradictions instead.

01Schedule
Inconsistency
Critical severityCode: ERR-OPS-01

Opening hours contradict themselves across pages

Two pages on your own site state two different opening schedules. A client checking before a Sunday visit gets one answer from the pricing page and the opposite from the massage page — and Google reads both.

Verified URLs: melzabeautyspa.co.za/massage-spa-soweto-prices-guide/ vs melzabeautyspa.co.za/full-body-massage-soweto/

What the pages actually say

Source A — Pricing page

Mon–Fri: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM
Saturday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Sunday: Closed

Source B — Massage & treatments pages

Tue–Sun: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Monday: Closed
Sunday: Open full day

Why it costs bookings

A prospective Sunday client who checks the pricing page assumes you are shut and books elsewhere. A Monday client who trusts the massage page arrives to closed doors. Either way, the contradiction is live on the pages Google shows first — and local search rewards businesses whose hours agree everywhere.

Fix: one source of truth for hours — the same schedule on every page, the footer, and the Google Business Profile. Status: Live conflict unresolved

02Stale Content
Artifact
High severityCode: OBS-MKT-02

Expired specials still live on homepage

Your homepage still advertises a seasonal special that ended more than a year ago. A second special on the same page carries no year at all, so a visitor cannot tell whether it is current.

Verified URL: melzabeautyspa.co.za/

Raw page capture evidence

“Spring Glow Special — Valid 1st–30th September 2025”

Observed active and prominent on the homepage in November 2026 — more than a year past its validity window. A separate “September Special R400 — valid until the end of November” states no year, so its status cannot be verified from the page.

Why it costs bookings

First-time visitors judge a business by its homepage. An expired offer in the hero area tells them the site is not maintained — and if the offers are stale, they wonder whether the prices are too. The homepage's best real estate is spent on a discount nobody can claim.

Fix: remove the expired special and replace it with an evergreen booking prompt; date every future offer with its year. Status: Live on homepage

03Content
Duplication
Medium severityCode: DOM-DUP-03

Duplicated Couples Bliss package block

The entire Couples Bliss package block appears twice on the homepage, word for word — the same offer, the same price, rendered a second time further down the page.

Verified URL: melzabeautyspa.co.za/

Both occurrences

Occurrence #1 — mid-page section

“Couples Bliss Package — 60min Full Body Hot Stone Massage + Foot Scrub (R799)”

Occurrence #2 — further down the same page

“Couples Bliss Package — 60min Full Body Hot Stone Massage + Foot Scrub (R799)”

Why it costs bookings

On a phone, the duplicate block doubles the scroll length of your most valuable page and makes the site look unfinished. Search engines also see the same content twice on one URL, which dilutes the page instead of strengthening it.

Fix: keep one Couples Bliss block; remove the duplicate. Status: Live on homepage

04Typography
Run-on
Medium severityCode: TYP-ERR-04

Garbled headline copy in couples section

A headline in the couples section reads as two sentences run together, with a word broken mid-way. It is visible to every visitor who scrolls to your most romantic offer.

Verified URL: melzabeautyspa.co.za/ (couples section)

Literal browser render

“Reconnect & Relax Together in Meadowlandsying quality time together”

The headline reads as a copy or layout slip — two phrases joined without a break — visible on the live page.

Why it costs bookings

Couples packages (R799) and the Midweek 2-for-1 (R500) are emotional purchases — gifts, anniversaries, dates. A garbled headline on exactly that offer breaks the mood at the moment a visitor is deciding whether to book.

Fix: re-author the headline as one clean sentence. Status: Live on the page

05Indexation
Failure
Medium severityCode: JS-CRAWL-05

“Loading your guides...” blank JS block

The Melza Glow Guide blog section depends on JavaScript to render. Until that script runs, the section shows only a loading message — and for search crawlers, the articles behind it may as well not exist.

Verified URL: melzabeautyspa.co.za/blog/

What a crawler sees before JavaScript runs

Loading your guides...

The blog grid renders only after client-side JavaScript executes. If the script fails — or for a crawler that does not wait — the section is empty: no article titles, no links, nothing indexed.

Why it costs bookings

You published 10+ substantive articles on hyper-pigmentation and body treatments, updated as recently as 20 October 2026. That is real content work — but if search engines cannot read the articles, they cannot send you the readers, and readers cannot become bookings.

Fix: render the blog archive as static, pre-rendered HTML so crawlers and visitors see the articles with or without JavaScript. Status: Live on /blog

06NAP Citation
Drift
Medium severityCode: GEO-NAP-06

Address spelling drifts between pages

Your street name is spelled two different ways on your own site. To a local search algorithm, two spellings can look like two different businesses at two different addresses.

Verified URL: melzabeautyspa.co.za/contact-us/ and other pages on the same site

The two spellings, on your own pages

Most pages & footer

“30087 Tshendehulu Street”

With an “h” after “Ts”.

Another page on the same site

“30087 Tsendehulu Str”

Missing the “h”, abbreviated “Str”.

Why it costs bookings

Local search ranks businesses on consistency: the same name, address and phone everywhere. When your own pages disagree on the street name, Google's confidence in your location drops — and with it, your place in the local results for “spa Soweto” searches.

Fix: standardise on “30087 Tshendehulu Street” everywhere — pages, footer, schema, and every listing. Status: Trivial fix, large local payoff

07Conversion
Brake
High severityCode: SOC-PRF-07

Fresha 4.4★ rating hidden from your own visitors

Your Fresha profile carries a 4.4★ rating from 5 public reviews — real social proof, already earned. None of it appears anywhere on your own website.

Evidence: Fresha public profile (Melza_Beauty), checked 5 Nov 2026

Verified Fresha record

4.4★ (5 public reviews)

Zero badges, embeds, or review markup appear on melzabeautyspa.co.za. Two review snippets are visible on Fresha: “Great experience” (29 Jun 2025) and “Experience was lovely, very friendly staff members and a very clean place” (29 Aug 2026).

Competitor vulnerability

Nearby Soweto salons on the same Fresha pages show 4.9★ from 39–83 reviews — displayed where searching clients compare them, before they ever reach your site.

Why it costs bookings

High-value bookings — IV vitamin infusions (R800–R1,300), multi-hour bridal journeys — need trust before price. A visitor comparing a 4.4★ spa with no visible reviews against a 4.9★ spa with dozens has already made the decision.

Fix: embed the verified Fresha rating above the booking path and add review markup so the stars can appear in search results. Status: Proof exists, unused

08Dead-End
Funnel
High severityCode: FUN-DRA-08

10+ articles last updated 20 Oct 2026 with no booking path

Your blog is the most active part of the site — 10+ published editorial pieces, updated as recently as 20 October 2026. A reader who finishes one reaches the bottom of the page and finds no direct way to book.

Verified URL: melzabeautyspa.co.za/blog/

What is missing from the articles

Missing element 1

Sticky booking button — no booking action follows the reader down the page.

Missing element 2

Structured FAQ — no structured answers found on the pages checked, so AI search has nothing to quote.

Missing element 3

Local anchors — no links tying articles back to Meadowlands and Soweto service pages.

What the content already does

10+ articles on skin and body treatments, actively maintained — the hard part is done.

Why it costs bookings

Readers who find your articles through search are the warmest visitors you get — they already trust your expertise. Without a booking path on the page, that trust has nowhere to go, and the visit ends at the bottom of the article.

Fix: add a sticky booking button and structured FAQ answers to every article, and link each one to its matching treatment page. Status: Content live, conversion missing
